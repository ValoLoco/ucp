# Decision 0001: Research before infrastructure

Status: proposed baseline for review.

## Context

The original seven-layer architecture combined legal ambitions with technology selections before validating their necessity or feasibility.

## Direction

Use Bitcoin-first as a design hypothesis. Start with a portable, signed entity record. Keep identity, legal recognition, contract interpretation, payment execution, and dispute enforcement distinct. Select dependencies only after comparing existing standards and documenting trust assumptions.

## Consequences

No mandatory Stacks dependency, tokenized cap table, protocol token, or on-chain arbitration in the initial demonstrator. Later layers remain research topics. This reduces scope while preserving the broader vision.
