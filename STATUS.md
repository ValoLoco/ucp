# Project status

UCP is an early-stage research proposal developed publicly through BUENATURA. No participating jurisdictions, institutional endorsements, legal recognition, deployed protocol, or production readiness are claimed.

Read this document alongside README.md and docs/architecture.md. Those initial documents describe an aspirational architecture, not verified capabilities or commitments. Where they conflict with this status or the research notes, treat the earlier claims as superseded.

Bitcoin-first is the research direction. Stacks, ION, Taproot Assets, RGB, Lightning, and BitVM are candidates to evaluate, not required dependencies. The seven layers are a problem map, not seven products to build immediately.

The first milestone is a signed, portable entity record with independently verifiable history. It does not create a legal person or issue securities.

No protocol token is proposed. Funding, stewardship, and final licensing require explicit decisions before implementation or external contributions.
